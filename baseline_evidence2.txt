### **Executive Decision: The "Stability-First" Pilot Pivot**

We are immediately implementing a portfolio rebalancing and tightening of underwriting standards to align with our 5.5% EDR mandate. 

*   **Customer Segment Mix:** Shift to a defensive posture: **55% Service SMEs** (Anchor), **25% Retail**, and **20% Manufacturing**.

*   **Risk Limits:** 15% safety margin on internal scoring. Automatic "Credit Freeze" triggered if any segment exceeds 85% of its default ceiling.
*   **Go-To-Market Approach:** Pivot from broad-reach to hyper-targeted, industry-specific lead aggregation to ensure the Approval-to-Funded ratio remains healthy under tighter screening.
*   **Implementation Sequence:** 
    *   **Days 1–15:** System integration of enhanced underwriting and automated rejection protocols.
    *   **Days 16–30:** Full deployment of revised pricing and segment-specific marketing.
    *   **Ongoing:** Daily monitoring of Approval-to-Disbursement ratios; weekly Treasury audits.
