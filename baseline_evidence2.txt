### **Executive Decision: The "Quality-First" Pivot**

#### **1. Customer Segment Mix**
We are rebalancing the portfolio to reduce volatility and prioritize risk-adjusted returns:
*   **Service SMEs:** 50% (Primary growth engine).
*   **Retail Shops:** 30% (Reduced exposure).
*   **Small Manufacturers:** 20% (Stable).

#### **2. Product Terms & Pricing**
*   **Interest Rates:** 19% for Retail and Manufacturing; 17% for Service SMEs.
*   **Pricing Floor:** 19% is the non-negotiable floor for high-risk segments; no discounts without Treasury sign-off.
*   **Loan Characteristics:** Standardized ticket size of INR 4.5 Lakh with mandatory cash-flow verification.

#### **3. Approval Policy & Risk Limits**
*   **Hard Stop:** Any segment exceeding a 5.5% default rate will trigger an immediate credit freeze.
*   **Underwriting:** Implementation of the **DRAC (Dynamic Risk-Adjusted Ceiling)** model.
*   **Verification:** Mandatory API-based income verification and biometric liveness checks for all applicants.
*   **DTI Caps:** Tiered limits (40%/35%/25%) based on risk profile.

#### **4. Budget Allocation**
*   **Liquidity Buffer:** 5% (INR 1.35 Cr) ring-fenced immediately.
*   **Marketing Spend:** 50% Search/Retargeting (Efficiency), 35% Social/Partnerships (Scale), 15% Testing (Innovation).
*   **CAC Discipline:** Shift focus from "Total Volume" to "Cost per Quality Lead."

#### **5. Go-To-Market (GTM) Approach**
*   **Messaging:** "Credit-First" marketing to pre-filter unqualified leads.
*   **Funnel Optimization:** Prioritize a 15% increase in landing page conversion to offset the 25% reduction in eligible demand.

#### **6. Implementation Sequence (30-Day Horizon)**
*   **Days 1–10:** Update LMS pricing, deploy API-based income verification, and implement "Credit-First" ad copy.
*   **Days 11–20:** Shift marketing spend to Service SME lookalike audiences and mandate secondary business evidence.
*   **Days 21–30:** Establish the bi-weekly "Risk-Yield" dashboard and conduct the first portfolio audit.

#### **7. Measurable Outcomes**
*   **Default Rate:** Maintain an aggregate default rate ≤ 5.5%.
*   **Net Spread:** Sustain a net spread of 0.8%–1.0% post-provisioning.
*   **Conversion Efficiency:** Improve landing page conversion rates by 15% to maintain target volumes despite stricter underwriting.
*   **Portfolio Health:** Achieve a 100% compliance rate with the new "Credit-First" underwriting rules.
