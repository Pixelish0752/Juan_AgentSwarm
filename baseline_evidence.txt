Scenario:  A major domestic generic competitor unexpectedly drops their market price by 40% on the day of launch for a blockbuster cardiovascular drug, threatening initial volume capture.


               FINAL CEO DECISION                 
**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Executive Leadership Team
**FROM:** CEO
**DATE:** October 27, 2023
**SUBJECT:** FINAL DECISION: Strategic Response to Cardiovascular Portfolio Competition

After reviewing the competing mandates from Finance, Marketing, and Compliance, I am setting our course. We are in a high-stakes market entry, and while I appreciate the CMO’s desire for aggressive growth, we will not sacrifice our long-term financial health or regulatory standing for a "scorched earth" battle. 

### **The Final Decision: "Reliability-First" Market Defense**
We will adopt a **Value-Preservation Strategy.** We will not engage in a 40% price match, nor will we pursue a broad-based, high-burn market saturation play. Our competitive advantage is our reputation for quality and supply chain consistency. We will win by being the "stable, reliable partner" when the competitor’s aggressive pricing inevitably leads to supply chain fragility or quality issues.

### **Rejected Alternative: "Market Saturation" (CMO Proposal)**
I am formally rejecting the CMO’s proposal for a "Volume-First" rebate structure and broad-based PAP expansion. 
*   **Reasoning:** This approach treats the product as a pure commodity and would lead to unsustainable margin erosion. Furthermore, it ignores the Compliance mandate regarding predatory pricing risks and the Finance mandate regarding margin protection. We will not subsidize current volume at a lower margin; we will focus our resources on protecting our existing base and capturing high-value, high-loyalty segments.

### **Trade-offs**
*   **Short-term vs. Long-term:** We are trading immediate, aggressive market share gains for long-term margin stability and brand integrity. We accept that we may lose some price-sensitive, low-margin accounts in the short term.
*   **Operational Rigor:** We are trading the flexibility of a "growth-at-all-costs" budget for the security of a "Quality-First" mandate. This limits our ability to react impulsively but protects us from the catastrophic regulatory and quality risks identified by Compliance.

### **Strategic Directives**
1.  **Finance:** Implement the "Switch-Protection" coupon program immediately. It must be data-driven and focused on high-risk accounts. Finance will provide the maximum rebate tiers by EOD.
2.  **Operations/Supply Chain:** Maintain "Just-in-Time" manufacturing. No inventory stockpiling. We will rely on our existing, efficient supply chain to outlast the competitor.
3.  **Compliance:** All commercial and pricing discussions must include Legal counsel. The "Quality-First" mandate is in effect; no shortcuts in API sourcing or manufacturing.

### **Measurable Business KPIs**
To track the success of this strategy, we will monitor the following:

1.  **Net Realized Price (NRP) Stability:** We will track our average net price per unit. Success is defined as maintaining at least 90% of our pre-launch margin despite the competitive pressure.
2.  **Customer Retention Rate (Account-Level):** We will measure the percentage of existing high-volume accounts retained. We are targeting a retention rate of >85% for our top-tier accounts.
3.  **Competitor Supply Reliability Index:** We will track the competitor’s fill rates and backorder notifications. Our success is correlated to their inability to maintain supply; we aim to capture 100% of the "overflow" volume caused by their potential stock-outs.
