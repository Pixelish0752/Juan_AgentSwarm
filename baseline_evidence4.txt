### **Executive Decision: The "Hybrid Scaled" Operational Pivot**

To maintain our 48-hour SLA, ensure 100% regulatory compliance, and preserve liquidity, we are adopting the **Hybrid Scaled Model** effective immediately.

*   **Customer Segment Mix:** Focus on prime and near-prime applicants to ensure high-intent, lower-risk profiles that align with our new 40% manual verification requirement.
*   **Product Terms:** Standardized personal loan products with clear APR disclosures; no changes to existing interest rates to maintain competitive positioning.
*   **Approval Policy:** Shift to a 60/40 (Automated/Manual) verification split. All applications must pass automated KYC/KYB checks; the 40% manual review will focus on high-risk flags and complex income verification to ensure fair lending compliance.
*   **Budget Allocation:** Total investment of INR 10.7 Lakh. INR 8 Lakh (CapEx) for automated integration; INR 2.7 Lakh (OpEx) for temporary staffing. The remaining INR 4.3 Lakh is reserved as a liquidity buffer.
*   **Risk Limits:** Implementation of "Hard-Stop" underwriting rules: DTI ratio capped at 40%; automatic decline for applicants failing automated identity verification.
*   **Go-To-Market (GTM) Approach:** Shift marketing spend toward high-intent, organic, and referral channels to ensure lead quality, reducing the burden on the verification team.
*   **Implementation Sequence:** 
    *   **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** Onboard 2 temporary reviewers to clear the backlog and manage the 40% manual requirement.
    *   **Phase 2 (Weeks 1-2):** Initiate Automated Verification Service integration (milestone-based payments).
    *   **Phase 3 (Week 3+):** Transition to automated workflows; phase out temporary staff as efficiency gains are realized.
