               FINAL CEO DECISION                 
As CEO of FinNova Capital, I have synthesized the inputs from our departments to finalize our operational and strategic roadmap. We will proceed with the **"Hybrid-Automation" Model**, prioritizing infrastructure over headcount to ensure long-term scalability and financial discipline.

### **Executive Decision: The FinNova Growth & Compliance Framework**

#### **1. Customer Segment Mix**
*   **Primary Focus:** Established SMEs with proven cash flow.
*   **Targeting:** Shift marketing messaging from "Fast Cash" to "Growth Capital for Established Businesses" to self-select for higher-quality, lower-risk applicants.

#### **2. Product Terms**
*   **Structure:** Standardized B2B lending products.
*   **Transparency:** All APRs, origination fees, and repayment schedules must be presented in plain language at the point of application.
*   **Eligibility:** DTI $\le 36\%$ (General) / $\le 30\%$ (High-Growth); LTV $\le 80\%$ for secured products.

#### **3. Approval Policy**
*   **Verification:** Mandatory API-based automated income verification for all loans >$10,000.
*   **Manual Review:** Triggered only for the 40% of applications identified as "edge cases" or where stated/verified income variance exceeds 10%.
*   **Fraud:** Multi-factor identity verification (biometric + device fingerprinting) is mandatory for all new accounts.

#### **4. Budget Allocation**
*   **Total Budget:** INR 15 Lakh (3-month window).
*   **CAPEX:** INR 8 Lakh allocated to automated verification integration.
*   **Contingency:** INR 7 Lakh held in a high-liquidity reserve for licensing or unforeseen technical hurdles.
*   **Hiring:** **Freeze.** No temporary staff will be hired.

#### **5. Risk Limits**
*   **Default Ceiling:** Strictly $\le 4\%$ per annum.
*   **Portfolio Controls:** 40% Retail/Consumer, 35% SME/Commercial, 25% High-Growth/Tech.
*   **Trigger:** If aggregate defaults exceed 3.5%, the system will automatically trigger a "Tightening Phase" (increasing minimum credit score requirements by 20 points).

#### **6. Go-To-Market (GTM) Approach**
*   **Tiered Intent Strategy:** 50% SEM (High Intent), 20% Retargeting, 20% Affiliate/Partners, 10% Content/Social.
*   **Lead Quality:** Implement a 3-question eligibility screener on landing pages to filter out unqualified leads before they enter the funnel.

#### **7. Implementation Sequence**
*   **Weeks 1–2:** Integration of automated verification software; training of existing 8 reviewers on the new system.
*   **Weeks 3–12:** Full deployment of the "Hybrid-Automation" model. Utilize surplus manual capacity to implement a "Fast-Track" queue for high-value/repeat borrowers.

#### **8. Measurable Outcomes**
*   **Operational:** Maintain a median approval time of $\le 48$ hours.
*   **Compliance:** Keep customer complaint rates below 2%.
*   **Financial:** Achieve a downward trend in Cost-per-Approval by Month 3.
*   **Risk:** Maintain portfolio default rate $\le 4\%$ and ensure fraud losses remain within the 0.5% allocated buffer.

**Final Directive:** This strategy preserves our liquidity, satisfies our compliance mandate, and optimizes our operational throughput without increasing fixed costs. 
All department heads are to align their KPIs with these parameters effective immediately.
