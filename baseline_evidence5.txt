               FINAL CEO DECISION                 
As CEO of FinNova Capital, I have synthesized the departmental inputs to address our current portfolio challenges. We are moving from a reactive stance to a **"Controlled Deployment"** model to preserve capital and ensure long-term viability amidst rising costs and fraud risks.

### **Executive Decision: Portfolio Re-optimization**

*   **Customer Segment Mix:** 40% Retail (High-Yield/High-Touch) / 60% Non-Retail (Low-Risk/Institutional).
*   **Product Terms:** Retail pricing set at **19% APR** to offset the 13% Cost of Funds (CoF) and fraud-related leakage. Non-Retail yield target remains at 17.5%.
*   **Approval Policy:** "Screen-First" mandate. All retail applications must pass automated fraud screening (Device Fingerprinting, Velocity Checks, and Biometric verification). Manual underwriting is mandatory for all high-value or borderline cases.
*   **Budget Allocation:** Total deployable capital is **INR 21 Cr** (after maintaining the INR 3 Cr liquidity buffer). Retail: INR 8.4 Cr; Non-Retail: INR 12.6 Cr.
*   **Risk Limits:** Aggregate portfolio default ceiling is **5.5%**. A "Management Buffer" of 15% below this ceiling is established; hitting this buffer triggers an automatic tightening of credit scoring models.
*   **Go-To-Market Approach:** Shift marketing focus to high-intent, low-funnel channels to ensure lead quality. Implement "Credit-Gate" filtering at the application stage to prevent wasted acquisition costs.
*   **Implementation Sequence:** 
    1. Immediate integration of the Fraud Screening API (Priority 1).
    2. Update internal risk models to reflect 13% CoF and new fraud parameters.
    3. Rebalance portfolio allocation to the 40/60 split.
    4. 30-day post-launch audit to verify fraud reduction targets.
*   **Measurable Outcomes:** Restoration of a 3.6% net interest spread and stabilization of the default rate below the 5.5% threshold.

---

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

1.  **Fraud Incidence Rate:** Must be reduced from 7% to **$\leq$ 2.8%** within 30 days of implementing the "Screen-First" policy.
2.  **Net Interest Margin (NIM):** Maintain a minimum spread of **3.6%** (Weighted Yield of 18.1% vs. 14.5% break-even cost).
3.  **Portfolio Default Rate:** Must remain strictly below the **5.5%** ceiling; any breach triggers an immediate freeze on new retail disbursements.
